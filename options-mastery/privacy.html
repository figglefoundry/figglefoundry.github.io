Options Mastery – Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026 · Developer: Figgle Foundry, Singapore · Contact: figglefoundry@gmail.com

In short: Options Mastery does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. Your progress stays on your phone, and payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

1. Who we are

Options Mastery ("the app") is an educational course about options trading, published on Google Play by Figgle Foundry, an individual developer based in Singapore. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no sign-up or login, contains no advertising, and includes no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. We do not run any servers that receive data from the app.

3. Information stored on your device

To make the course work, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • lessons completed, quiz results, badges and review cards;
  • the trading plan and exit rules you write in the app;
  • settings such as theme and sound preferences;
  • whether the full course has been unlocked on this device.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

4. Purchases and promo codes

The full course can be unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase, or with a promo code redeemed on Google Play. Purchases and promo codes are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or receive your card or payment details.

When you buy or restore the unlock, Google Play tells the app on your device that the purchase is valid so the full course can open. As a seller, Google provides us with standard order records for purchases made through Google Play, such as the order number, date, product and country, which we use only for accounting, refunds and customer support. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Device permissions

  • Vibration: for light haptic feedback when you answer questions.
  • Network access and Google Play billing: used only by Google Play to process and verify purchases. The course content itself is built into the app and works offline.

6. Contacting us

If you email us, we will receive your email address and anything you include in the message. We use it only to reply to you and keep it no longer than needed for that purpose. You may ask us to delete our correspondence with you at any time.

7. Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data from the app, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have contacted us by email, you can ask us about, correct or delete that correspondence, including under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012, by writing to the address below.

9. Educational use only

The app teaches how options work. It does not provide investment advice, does not connect to any brokerage, and all tickers, prices and results in it are simulated.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
